How can we help?

Learn how to use the main features of your Student Task Manager.

Managing Tasks

Use My Tasks to manage all your academic tasks.

  • Create a new task.
  • Add title, description, priority and deadline.
  • Edit existing tasks.
  • Mark tasks as completed.
  • Delete tasks when they are no longer needed.

Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a quick overview of your academic work.

  • View total tasks.
  • Check completed tasks.
  • See pending tasks.
  • Check upcoming deadlines.

Analytics

Analytics helps you understand your task performance.

  • View task completion rate.
  • Analyze completed and pending tasks.
  • Monitor your academic progress.

Profile

Manage your personal student information from Profile.

  • View your profile.
  • Click Edit Profile to edit your information.
  • Click Save Changes to save your updates.

Settings

Customize your Student Task Manager preferences.

  • Enable or disable Dark Mode.
  • Enable Task Notifications.
  • Enable Task Reminders.
  • Enable Email Reminders.
  • Select default task priority and view.

Forgot Password

If you forget your password, you can reset it from the login page.

  • Open the login page.
  • Click Forgot Password.
  • Enter your email address.
  • Enter your security answer if requested.
  • Enter and confirm your new password.

Quick Help
